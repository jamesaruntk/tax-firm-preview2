Getting started

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Last year’s tax return, a photo ID, and any tax forms you’ve received this year — like W-2s, 1099s, and 1098s. After booking, you’ll get a personalized checklist.

Do I need to meet in person?

No. Meetings can be in person in the Hyattsville area or online by video. Documents are shared through a secure portal either way.Confirm

Taxes and deadlines

When is the filing deadline?

For most individuals, federal returns are due April 15. If that date falls on a weekend or holiday, it moves to the next business day. An extension gives you more time to file, but not more time to pay.

What are estimated taxes?

If you earn income that doesn’t have tax withheld — like self-employment or rental income — the IRS expects payments during the year, usually four times. Paying on time helps avoid penalties.

Can you help with past-year returns I never filed?

Yes. Prior-year returns can be prepared and filed, and a plan can be made to handle any balance owed.

IRS notices and privacy

I got a letter from the IRS. What should I do?

Don’t ignore it, and don’t panic. Most notices ask for a simple correction or more information. Check the response date on the letter, then reach out so it can be reviewed with you.

How is my personal information protected?

Sensitive documents are exchanged only through a secure, encrypted portal. Please don’t send Social Security numbers or account numbers by email or through the contact form on this site.
